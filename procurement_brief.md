# Sample Procurement Brief: Molded Automotive Floor Mats

**Exercise type:** Request for quotation (RFQ) for a simple automotive interior component  
**Buyer:** Pravaah Mobility Works (fictional)  
**Vehicle:** Pravaah P1 small hatchback (fictional)  
**Issue date:** 18 October 2026  
**Currency:** Indian rupees (INR)

> Training scenario only. The buyer, vehicle, supplier companies, locations, quotes, capabilities, and experience are fictional. They are not real companies or market quotes.

## 1. Requirement

Pravaah Mobility Works invites quotations to design, tool, sample, and supply one **four-piece molded TPE floor-mat set** (two front mats and two rear mats) for the Pravaah P1 cabin. This product was selected for a beginner exercise because its use, shape, material, one-time tooling cost, piece price, fitment sample, and delivery plan are easy to compare.

### Basic product specification

- Black, cleanable thermoplastic elastomer (TPE), nominal body thickness around 3 mm; supplier to declare actual grade and tolerance.
- Four pieces per saleable set, shaped from the buyer-supplied vehicle floor template and retention-point samples.
- Textured anti-slip underside; raised perimeter around 8-12 mm; reinforced driver's heel zone.
- No sharp edges, loose inserts, or interference with pedals, seat travel, or the supplied retention features.
- Supplier to propose a compound/material data sheet, sample fitment plan, packaging, and manufacturing inspection controls.
- Exact fitment drawing, sample carpet, and retention clips are issued to shortlisted suppliers after confidentiality and access arrangements.

## 2. Planning quantities

- 20 fitment samples for initial approval.
- 200 sets for a pilot lot after first-article approval.
- Indicative annual forecast: 10,000 sets. Forecast is non-binding; only released purchase orders are firm.
- Quote unit price per four-piece set, one-time tooling separately, GST separately, and freight to the buyer's Pune-area plant separately.
- State monthly capacity and lead time from receipt of approved drawings, sample, and tooling PO.

## 3. Supplier response format

Each response should include:

1. Company overview and relevant automotive component experience.
2. TPE grade, construction, thickness, anti-slip feature, edge, heel reinforcement, and retention design.
3. Tool concept, ownership, maintenance, sample timing, fitment process, and included correction rounds.
4. Quality plan: incoming material, first-article approval, dimensional and visual checks, lot traceability, and non-conformance handling.
5. Commercial table: unit price, one-time tooling, GST, freight, payment terms, validity, exclusions, and escalation assumptions.
6. Schedule for sample, pilot, and production; monthly capacity and delivery cadence.
7. Warranty duration, claims response, containment, replacement timing, and support contact.
8. Risks or buyer decisions required before award.

## 4. Example evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to examine |
|---|---:|---|
| Product & Tooling Capability | 25% | Fitment plan, mould approach, material/process understanding, retention, and production readiness |
| Quality & Compliance | 25% | Compound traceability, first article, control plan, dimensional checks, defect containment, and evidence |
| Commercial Value | 20% | Set price plus tooling, GST/freight clarity, assumptions, and total cost |
| Delivery & Capacity | 20% | Sample and pilot schedule, monthly capacity, correction allowance, and delivery cadence |
| Warranty & Supplier Support | 10% | Warranty, claim response, replacement, named support, and relevant experience |

Scores use a 0-10 scale. These weights are seeded in the demo SQLite database and can be changed in the UI so long as active criteria total 100%.
